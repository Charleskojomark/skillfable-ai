# SkillFable AI

> **"Learn AI. Live the story. Build real skills."**

SkillFable AI is an immersive, story-driven learning platform that teaches practical artificial intelligence skills to students, professionals, freelancers, and entrepreneurs in Nigeria and across Africa.

Learners don't just watch videos or answer disconnected multiple-choice quizzes. They enter fictional stories, make high-stakes decisions, complete practical technical missions, and build a verified portfolio of real-world deliverables.

---

## 🌟 Season 1: The Intelligence Project

### The Premise
You are accepted as an AI Fellow at **Relay Studio**, a small, ambitious innovation team helping African organizations solve real-world problems with AI.
During your first assignment preparing a pilot demonstration for a health cooperative, an unverified message arrives:

> *"Don’t show us another tool that works only in a demonstration. Show us what happens when it fails."*

Your mission: uncover the meaning behind that message, solve real failures in data, prompting, security, and algorithms, and build AI systems worth trusting.

### Core Characters
* **Amara Eze** — Product & Operations Lead (Lagos)
* **Kojo Mensah** — Research & Data Analyst (Accra)
* **Zuri Kamau** — Engineer & Builder (Nairobi)
* **Ibrahima Sow** — Community Partnerships & Field Lead (Dakar)
* **The Fellow (You)** — Protagonist & Decision Maker

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom Afro-Tech Cinematic tokens (`#0e1321` Midnight Navy, `#ffc174` Warm Amber, `#6bd8cb` Muted Teal)
* **Typography**: Newsreader (Editorial Serif) & Plus Jakarta Sans (Modern UI)
* **Icons**: Google Material Symbols Outlined
* **State & Persistence**: LocalStorage state management
* **Audio**: Web Audio API ambient synthesizer simulation
* **Deployment**: Vercel
